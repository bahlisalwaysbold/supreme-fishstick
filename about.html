About LTE House

Built by engineers who got tired of unreliable power.

LTE House started as a two-person installation crew and grew into a full-service solar engineering company — because every project we touched needed less "sales" and more actual engineering.

Our story

From one rooftop to a full engineering practice.

Founded in 2010, LTE House was created to close the gap between "solar salesmen" and real electrical engineering. What began with a handful of residential installs is now a team of certified engineers and technicians delivering systems for homes, offices and industrial sites — with the same attention to detail on every project, big or small.

Founder & CEO

Ahmad Tijjani Nuhu Founder & Chief Executive Officer, LTE House
