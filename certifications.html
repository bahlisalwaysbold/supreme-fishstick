Why it matters

Certifications protect your warranty, not just our reputation.

  • Manufacturer warranties are usually void if installed by uncertified technicians.
  • Compliant wiring reduces fire risk and insurance disputes.
  • Certified system design means accurate sizing — no under- or over-spec'd systems.
